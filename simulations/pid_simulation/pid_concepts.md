# PID Simulation Concepts

## What is PID?

PID is a feedback-based control system used to maintain stable robotic movement.

PID:
- Proportional
- Integral
- Derivative

---

# Why PID Matters

PID helps:
- stabilize motion
- reduce oscillation
- maintain heading
- correct movement errors

---

# Planned PID Applications

EcoFin may later use PID for:
- depth stabilization
- directional correction
- movement balancing
- autonomous navigation
