# Thruster Control Simulation

## Objective

This simulation area focuses on understanding how multiple thrusters interact during underwater movement.

---

# Thruster Configuration

EcoFin currently uses:
- left fin thruster
- right fin thruster
- rear tail thruster

---

# Simulated Movement Logic

## Forward Movement
All thrusters generate forward thrust.

---

## Turning Logic
Differential thrust used for:
- left turns
- right turns
- directional correction

---

# Concepts Being Tested

- synchronized thrust
- PWM balancing
- directional movement
- thrust distribution
