# Underwater Propulsion Systems

## Introduction

Propulsion systems generate movement for underwater robotic systems.

EcoFin currently uses a thruster-based propulsion approach.

---

# Propulsion Types Researched

### Thruster-Based Systems
Advantages:
- simple control
- modular design
- precise movement

---

### Bio-Inspired Propulsion
Examples:
- fish tail motion
- fin movement systems

Advantages:
- natural movement
- energy efficiency

Challenges:
- complex mechanics
- advanced control requirements

---

# EcoFin Configuration

Current configuration:
- left fin thruster
- right fin thruster
- rear tail thruster

---

# Important Engineering Factors

- thrust efficiency
- underwater drag
- energy consumption
- directional stability.
