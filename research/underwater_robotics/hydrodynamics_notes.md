# Hydrodynamics Research Notes

## What is Hydrodynamics?

Hydrodynamics studies how fluids interact with moving objects.

For EcoFin, hydrodynamics affects:
- movement efficiency
- stability
- drag resistance
- energy usage

---

# Important Concepts Studied

### Drag
Water resistance acting against movement.

### Buoyancy
Upward force generated by water.

### Stability
Ability to maintain balanced movement underwater.

---

# Design Considerations

The body shape should:
- reduce drag
- improve movement efficiency
- maintain stable flow patterns
