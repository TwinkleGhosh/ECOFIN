# Underwater Robotics Overview

## Introduction

Underwater robotics focuses on robotic systems designed to operate in aquatic environments.

These systems are commonly used for:
- marine exploration
- surveillance
- environmental monitoring
- underwater inspection
- scientific research

---

# Importance of Underwater Robotics

Underwater robots can:
- access difficult environments
- perform autonomous operations
- collect environmental data
- assist in pollution management

---

# Types of Underwater Robots

### ROV
Remotely Operated Vehicle

Controlled by:
- wired communication
- human operator

---

### AUV
Autonomous Underwater Vehicle

Operates using:
- sensors
- navigation systems
- autonomous control algorithms

EcoFin is conceptually closer to an AUV system.

---

# Core Engineering Areas

- propulsion systems
- underwater navigation
- stabilization systems
