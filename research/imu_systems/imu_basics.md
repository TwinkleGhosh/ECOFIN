# IMU Basics

## What is an IMU?

IMU stands for:
Inertial Measurement Unit

It is used for:
- orientation tracking
- stabilization
- motion sensing

---

# Typical IMU Components

- accelerometer
- gyroscope
- magnetometer

---

# Importance in EcoFin

IMUs may later help:
- maintain heading
- stabilize movement
- detect orientation changes.
