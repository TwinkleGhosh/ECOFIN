# Sensor Fusion Research

## What is Sensor Fusion?

Sensor fusion combines multiple sensor readings to improve accuracy and reliability.

---

# Example Combination

- accelerometer
- gyroscope
- magnetometer

Combined together for:
- accurate orientation estimation
- movement tracking

---

# Why Sensor Fusion Matters

Individual sensors may contain:
- noise
- drift
- inaccuracies

Sensor fusion helps reduce these issues.

---

# Future Goals

- improved navigation accuracy
- stable movement estimation
- autonomous orientation tracking
