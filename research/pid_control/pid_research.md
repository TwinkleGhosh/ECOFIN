# PID Research Notes

## What is PID?

PID is a feedback-based control system widely used in robotics and automation.

PID:
- Proportional
- Integral
- Derivative

---

# Why PID is Important

PID helps stabilize:
- movement
- heading direction
- depth
- autonomous systems

---

# PID Components

## Proportional (P)
Corrects present error.

## Integral (I)
Corrects accumulated past error.

## Derivative (D)
Predicts future error trends.
