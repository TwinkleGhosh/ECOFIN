# Stabilization Theory

## Objective

Stable underwater movement is essential for:
- navigation
- sensor accuracy
- efficient propulsion

---

# Stabilization Areas

- roll stabilization
- pitch correction
- yaw correction
- drift reduction

---

# Planned Control Methods

Future systems may include:
- PID control
- sensor fusion
- autonomous correction logic

---

# Engineering Challenges

- underwater currents
- sensor noise
- unstable thrust
- vibration effects.
