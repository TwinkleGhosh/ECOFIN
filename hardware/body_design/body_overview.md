# Body Design Overview

## EcoFin Body Structure

EcoFin uses a PLA-based robotic fish body structure designed for:
- underwater movement
- modular hardware integration
- thruster mounting
- future sensor expansion

---

# Current Body Features

- fish-inspired structure
- lightweight PLA construction
- side fin thruster mounting
- rear tail propulsion support

---

# Design Goals

The body design aims to:
- minimize underwater drag
- improve movement stability
- support future electronics
- maintain modularity

---

# Engineering Concepts Studied

- hydrodynamics
- buoyancy considerations
- weight distribution
- waterproof compartment planning

---

Planned internal sections:
- battery compartment
