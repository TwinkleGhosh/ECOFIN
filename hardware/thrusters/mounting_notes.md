# Thruster Mounting Notes

## Mounting Objectives

Thrusters must be securely mounted to:
- reduce vibration
- maintain alignment
- improve hydrodynamic efficiency
- ensure waterproof cable routing

---

# Current Placement

## Left Fin Thruster
Mounted near:
- left side fin section

Purpose:
- directional support
- movement balancing

---

## Right Fin Thruster
Mounted near:
- right side fin section

Purpose:
- stabilization
- turning assistance

---

## Rear Tail Thruster
Mounted at:
- rear tail fin

Purpose:
- primary propulsion

---

# Structural Considerations

Important factors:
- PLA strength
- waterproof sealing
- cable protection
- vibration resistance
