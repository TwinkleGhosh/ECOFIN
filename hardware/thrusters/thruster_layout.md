# Thruster Layout Configuration

## EcoFin Propulsion Design

EcoFin uses a distributed 3-thruster architecture.

Layout:
- 1 left fin thruster
- 1 right fin thruster
- 1 rear tail thruster

---

# Movement Logic

## Forward Movement

All thrusters contribute to forward propulsion.

Primary thrust:
- rear tail thruster

Support stabilization:
- left fin thruster
- right fin thruster

---

## Turning Mechanism

Turning is achieved using differential thrust.

Example:
- increase left thruster speed
- reduce right thruster speed

This creates yaw rotation.

---

## Stability Control

Side thrusters help:
- maintain balance
- reduce drift
- stabilize movement
