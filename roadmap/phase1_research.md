# Phase 1 — Research & Foundations

## Objective

Build strong foundational understanding of underwater robotics concepts before hardware integration.

---

# Core Topics Studied

## Underwater Motion

EcoFin movement concepts:
- Surge → forward/backward
- Yaw → left/right turning

Future concepts:
- Heave → depth control

---

# Thruster Direction Logic

Basic movement concepts:

- Both thrusters same speed → move straight
- Left faster + right slower → turn right
- Right faster + left slower → turn left

---

# PWM Research

PWM controls ESC and thruster behavior.

Typical signal ranges:
- 1500 µs → neutral
- 1600–1900 µs → forward
- 1400–1100 µs → reverse
