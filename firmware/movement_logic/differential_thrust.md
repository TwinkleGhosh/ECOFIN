# Differential Thrust Concepts

## What is Differential Thrust?

Differential thrust is a movement technique where multiple thrusters operate at different speeds to create turning motion.

---

# EcoFin Thruster Configuration

EcoFin uses:
- left fin thruster
- right fin thruster
- rear tail thruster

---

# Turning Logic

## Left Turn
Reduce:
- left thruster speed

Increase:
- right thruster speed

---

## Right Turn
Reduce:
- right thruster speed

Increase:
- left thruster speed
