# ESC Learning Notes

## What is an ESC?

ESC (Electronic Speed Controller) regulates power delivery from the battery to brushless motors.

It converts PWM signals into motor movement .

---

## Role of ESC in EcoFin

ESCs are responsible for:
- controlling thruster speed
- enabling directional movement
- regulating acceleration
- stabilizing underwater propulsion

---

## Research Areas Covered

---

## Integration Workflow

Battery → ESC → Thruster

Microcontroller sends PWM signals to ESC.

ESC controls:
- motor speed
- direction
- thrust power
