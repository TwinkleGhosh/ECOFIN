# PID Control Notes

## What is PID?

PID stands for:
- Proportional
- Integral
- Derivative

It is a feedback control system widely used in robotics and autonomous systems.

---

## Why PID is Important

PID helps maintain:
- stable movement
- accurate direction
- smooth control
- automatic correction

---

## Basic Understanding

### Proportional
Corrects current error.

### Integral
Corrects accumulated past error.

### Derivative
Predicts future error trends.

---

- autonomous navigation
