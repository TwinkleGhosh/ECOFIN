# Thruster Research Notes

## Introduction

Thrusters are the primary propulsion system of EcoFin.

The project currently uses thruster-based movement instead of traditional robotic fish tail propulsion due to:
- higher control precision
- easier software implementation
- better stability for beginners
- modular scalability

---

## Research Topics Studied

### Types of Thrusters
- Brushless DC thrusters
- ROV thrusters
- Bidirectional thrusters

### Important Concepts
- thrust vectoring
- forward/reverse propulsion
- underwater drag
- hydrodynamic resistance
- buoyancy effects

---

## Thruster Control

Thrusters are generally controlled using:
- ESCs (Electronic Speed Controllers)
- PWM signals from microcontrollers

Typical movement:
- forward
- reverse
- yaw turning
- depth adjustment

---

## Challenges Identified
